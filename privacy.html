Privacy Policy

Rua Xe Long Ut Content Publisher · Last updated: 15 September 2026

1. Scope

This policy covers the internal publishing tool operated by Long Ut Car Wash to post the shop's own videos to the shop's own social media accounts.

2. Data we access

When the business owner connects a TikTok account, we receive an access token and the account's basic profile information (account name and open ID). We use these solely to publish videos to that same account.

3. Data we do NOT collect

  • We do not collect, store or process personal data of TikTok users, viewers or followers.
  • We do not read or analyse other people's TikTok content.
  • We run no advertising, tracking or profiling of any kind.

4. Storage

Access tokens are stored locally in a configuration file on the business owner's own computer. They are not transmitted to any third party or external server.

5. Retention and deletion

Tokens are kept only while the tool is in use. The business owner can delete them at any time by removing the configuration file, and can revoke the app's access at any time from TikTok's own settings.

6. Sharing

We do not sell, rent or share any data with third parties.

7. Children

The tool is not directed at children and processes no data about them.
